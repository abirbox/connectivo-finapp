SALARY/ATTENDANCE PORTAL NOW LIVES INSIDE FINOPS
=================================================
* Sidebar item "Salary/Attendance Portal" opens the full salary portal inside FinOps.
* Same login as FinOps (CEO, Developer, Admin accounts). No separate salary login.
* Data is stored in the same Cloudflare KV (keys start with "sal:"). Daily backups kept 60 days.
* Payroll Import reads finalized totals directly - SALARY_PORTAL_URL / KEY are no longer needed.

DEPLOY: unzip, then in the folder run:   npx wrangler deploy
(Works on Cloudflare's FREE plan: PDFs are created in your browser, the Worker only stores and emails them.)

MOVE EXISTING SALARY DATA: in your old salary portal open Settings > Backup and download the file,
then open the new Salary/Attendance Portal in FinOps > Settings > Restore and choose that file.
Re-enter your Brevo/Resend key in Settings > Email once (keys are stored in KV, never in the page).

SECURITY: README.md contains example passwords - do not keep real passwords in any file.

MOBILE APP MODE
On a phone the salary portal turns into an app: bottom tab bar, card lists, bottom-sheet forms,
pull-to-refresh, Share payslip PDF. Open https://<your-address>/salary/ on the phone and use
"Add to Home Screen" (iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen; Android: menu > Install app) to get an app icon.
